## Brand & Style

The visual identity channels the celestial drama of deep Diwali midnight punctured by luminous golden sparklers, diyas, and celebratory aerial bursts. Designed for an artisanal yet high-volume festive commerce experience, the interface pairs the mystique of deep cosmic violets with the intense warmth of molten amber gold.

The style is an avant-garde experimental dark mode: atmospheric night-sky surfaces layered with radiant golden embers, translucent dark-glass containers, and energetic, eccentric typography. It balances the awe and ritual joy of Indian festival traditions with rigorous e-commerce clarity—delivering instant trust for Sivakasi green crackers, safety compliance, bulk wholesale calculations, and seasonal family gifting.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the radiance of fire and sparklers against deep midnight velvet:

- **Primary (`#D97706` — Radiant Amber Gold):** Evokes marigold garlands, brass diyas, and sparkling fire trails. Used for primary conversion triggers, active states, cart actions, savings callouts, and luminous highlights.
- **Secondary (`#5B21B6` — Royal Violet Sky):** Represents the twilight heavens of festive nights. Applied to surface container gradients, category pill backgrounds, trust highlights, and decorative halos.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` — Peacock Green):** Reserved strictly for certified eco-friendly green cracker credentials, safety seals, in-stock availability indicators, and environmental claims.
- **Neutral (`#0F0B1E` — Midnight Obsidian):** A tinted black-violet serving as the base canvas, preventing sterile flat blacks and infusing warmth into the dark mode.

Supporting status tokens:
- **Discount & Urgency Red (`#EF4444`):** Lightning deals, festival countdown timers, and discount percentage pills.
- **Surface Elevation Containers:**
  - Canvas Base: `#0F0B1E`
  - Container Low: `#18122B`
  - Container Medium: `#231B3D`
  - Container High: `#2E2350`
  - Text High-Contrast: `#FFFBEB`
  - Text Muted: `#A79FC4`

## Typography

The type system creates tension between expressive festive celebration and systematic transactional legibility:

- **Syne (Headlines & Display):** Its geometric, flared letterforms capture kinetic sparkler paths and celebratory momentum. Used for hero announcements, festival sales headers, product titles, and box set naming.
- **Manrope (Body & Interface Labels):** A contemporary sans-serif with geometric precision and open apertures. Guarantees optimal scanability for technical specifications (decibel levels, shot counts, chemical compliance, unit pricing, and tiered wholesale ladders).

## Layout & Spacing

The interface employs an intentional 12-column responsive fluid grid built on an 8-point vertical cadence:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` screen margins. Max content container bounded at `1320px` to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins. Reflows product catalogs into 2 or 3-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins. Prioritizes full-width interactive cards, thumb-friendly bottom sheets, and horizontal swipe carousels for festive assortments.

Spacing rhythm strictly reinforces content priority: tight component padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) preserves density inside product cards, while expansive section separations (`space-xl`) grant atmospheric breathing room around featured sparkler showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons grey-tone shadows in favor of chromatic ambient glows and translucent obsidian glass:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0F0B1E` with subtle ambient radial gradients in deep violet (`rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.15)`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Product Tiles):** Solid fill `#18122B` bounded by a crisp 1px border (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** Surface shifts to `#231B3D`, accompanied by an ember bloom shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Drawers & Cart Dialogs):** Glassmorphic surface: `background: rgba(24, 18, 43, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.1)`.
- **Level 4 (Promo Modals & Flash Sale Floating Banners):** Floating elevated plane backed by an intense amber-violet halo: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 11, 30, 0.9), 0 0 24px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3`), mirroring organic sparks, fireworks rockets, and festive earthen diyas:

- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Generous full pill contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide high tactile affordance and inviting clickable touch targets.
- **Product Tiles & Promotional Containers:** Framed in `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii, smoothing the silhouette against the dark obsidian canvas.
- **Modals, Gift Box Banners & Feature Panels:** Anchor the visual rhythm with expansive `3rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer contours.
- **Cart Steppers & Quantity Adjusters:** Pill capsules enclosing circular micro-buttons to ensure accidental taps are eliminated on mobile touch screens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Festive Action:** Pill-shaped, background gradient from `#D97706` to `#F59E0B`, text `#0F0B1E` in `Manrope` weight 700. Glows with radiant amber shadow on hover.
- **Secondary (Wholesale / Quick View):** Translucent deep violet pill (`rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.3)`) bounded by a 1px border in amber (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.4)`), text `#FFFBEB`.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Ghost pill with muted violet text, transforming to solid `#231B3D` on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#18122B` with a 1px amber-gold ghost stroke and `rounded-lg` radius.
- **Header Badges:** Floating top pills indicating `% Off` in urgent crimson (`#EF4444`) and `Green Cracker Certified` in peacock green (`#10B981`).
- **Pricing Cluster:** Bold Syne numbers for current price, strike-through MRP in muted violet-grey, and total festive savings highlighted in golden amber.
- **Quantity Selector:** Integrated pill stepper (`-`, `number`, `+`) with micro-haptic feedback states.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Pill contours in `#231B3D` with subtle borders. Active state triggers a radiant gold border, rich amber fill (`#D97706`), and dark midnight typography.

### Safety & Certification Badges
- Compact capsules integrating micro-icons for `Low Smoke`, `100% Sivakasi Origin`, `QR Verification`, and `Child Friendly Decibels`. Filled with muted jade (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) and bordered with `#10B981`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Pill-curved inputs with `#18122B` backgrounds, recessed inset depth, and `#A79FC4` placeholder text. Focus state illuminates the perimeter with a 1.5px golden amber ring and soft outer glow.

### Wholesale Tier Table
- High-contrast matrix with surface `#231B3D`, contrasting price breaks (e.g., 25+ boxes, 50+ boxes) using vibrant golden typography and clear savings calculators.
