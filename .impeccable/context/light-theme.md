## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a radiant, modern Indian festive retail experience that balances joyful celebration with high-converting e-commerce clarity. Rooted in traditional festive energy yet expressed through contemporary corporate-modern restraint, the visual language establishes immediate consumer trust, premium appeal, and frictionless transactional speed.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Radiant & Auspicious:** Warm ivory and cream foundational surfaces set an inviting, celebratory tone without the overwhelming visual noise typical of seasonal retail.
- **Trustworthy & Compliant:** Deep royal purple paired with emerald green delivers a grounded sense of legitimacy, safety certification, and institutional compliance (essential for eco-friendly fireworks standards).
- **High-Converting Energy:** High-contrast saffron and gold actionable accents channel harvest excitement, urgency, and premium value.

### Design Movement: Modern Festive Commercial
A sophisticated fusion of **Contemporary Light Minimalism** and **Festive Warmth**. Rather than leaning into dark, smoky fireworks tropes, the UI employs radiant light surfaces, deliberate whitespace, crystalline typography, and jewel-tone focal points. Subtle golden-violet tinted borders and soft, diffused shadows elevate physical cracker boxes, gift hampers, and certifications into luxury festive items.

## Colors

The color palette is built strictly for high-conversion festive e-commerce, balancing rich royal jewel tones with warm neutral foundations and targeted urgency accents.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary (`#5B21B6` - Royal Deep Violet):** Anchor for navigation bars, prominent headings, primary badges, high-priority state borders, and royal brand moments. Conveys heritage, celebration, and premium status.
- **Primary Dark (`#4C1D95`):** Pressed states, high-contrast text on light tinted backgrounds.
- **Primary Container (`#F3E8FF`):** Violet-tinted surface containers, active filter pills, and subtle section backdrops.

### Accent & Functional Palette
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Festive Saffron Gold):** Conversion focal point. Primary Add-to-Cart buttons, festive highlight ribbons, star ratings, and celebratory alert banners. Paired with hover state `#B45309` and light surface container `#FEF3C7`.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Peacock Emerald Green):** Exclusively reserved for trust, green cracker CSIR-NEERI verification, eco-friendly certification stamps, stock confirmations, and positive transaction badges. Surface container: `#D1FAE5`.
- **Destructive / Urgency (`#DC2626` - Festive Crimson):** Flash deal countdowns, limited stock warnings, clearance price cross-outs, and discount badges (e.g., "70% OFF Festive Box"). Surface container: `#FEE2E2`.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas / Background (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Ivory):** Eliminates harsh blue-white screen fatigue and gives a welcoming, warm festive canvas.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure elevated cards, product panels, and modal trays to ensure packaging photography pops.
- **Surface Muted (`#F5F0E8` - Soft Cream):** Alternating table rows, filter sidebars, and card footer segments.
- **Text Primary (`#1C1917` - Deep Stone Charcoal):** 14.5:1 contrast against warm ivory, ensuring accessible readability for pricing and technical details.
- **Text Secondary (`#78716C` - Neutral Stone Gray):** Specifications, SKU details, inactive tabs, and secondary labels.
- **Border Ambient (`#E7E0D6`):** Delicate stone borders with an intentional 1px warm golden or faint violet undertone (`rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.08)`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate hierarchy between exuberant festive headlines and razor-sharp commerce utility.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric structure softened with warm, generous counters. It radiates modern optimism in large scale, making Diwali sales, mega-saver deals, and brand titles feel energetic without losing dignity.
- **Body & Numerical Labels (`Inter`):** Selected for industry-standard legibility in tabular figures, specs, safety instructions, and multilingual pricing. Indian rupee numerals (`₹`) must align cleanly with bold pricing values.
- **Currency & Discount Figures:** Product prices use `Plus Jakarta Sans 700` (`title-lg` or `headline-md`) alongside strikethrough original prices set in `Inter 400` (`body-md`) with neutral stone color. Discount tags always use uppercase `label-sm` with tight tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid optimized for browsing dense seasonal catalogs and making rapid impulse selections.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin: 2rem` (max-width container: 1320px), `gutter: 1.5rem`. E-commerce listings render 4 columns per row.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Listings render 3 columns per row.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product cards default to a 2-column compact grid, maximizing items visible above the fold.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Internal badge padding, star rating gaps, and inline price-to-discount distance.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Product card interior whitespace, cart drawer item rows, and form field inner clearance.
- **Section Rhythm (`space-xl` and higher):** Vertical separation between seasonal collections, customer review carousels, and safety compliance banners.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation bridges clean modern design with subtle festive warmth by tinting light shadows with soft royal violet and warm gold. Pure pitch-black drop shadows are prohibited.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas background (`#FAF8F5`). Flush components like promo banners, safety tables, and tab dividers.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px composite outline: `border: 1px solid #E7E0D6`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Quick Add):** Active card lift. `transform: translateY(-2px)`. Shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`. Border shifts to `rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Header & Dropdowns):** Sticky filter bars and mega menus. `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Cart Drawer, Quick View & Modals):** High-priority overlays. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.20)`. Modal overlay uses `#1C1917` with 45% opacity and a soft 3px blur to keep attention on the festive action.

## Shapes

The roundedness tier `2` sets a friendly, modern baseline (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius) that balances consumer warmth with structured retail density.

### Radius Distribution
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, quantity pickers, and filter pills.
- **Large (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Product cards, promo highlight cards, shopping cart panels, and modal containers.
- **Extra Large (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero promotional banners, festive gift hamper spotlights, and loyalty reward callouts.
- **Pill / Fully Rounded (9999px):** CSIR-NEERI green cracker certification badges, crimson discount tags, live chat widgets, and mini cart count indicators.
